One of the primary ethical concerns revolves around privacy and AI-driven moderation. The use of AI to detect and penalize toxic behavior requires collecting and analyzing in-game chats (both text and voice). However, this approach has the potential for false positives, bias in detection, and infringement on player privacy rights. One example of a real world case study is YouTube’s AI-based content moderation system. It has faced a lot of criticism for wrongful takedowns of videos due to AI errors. Back in 2020, YouTube admitted that its AI system, which was implemented more aggressively due to the pandemic, wrongfully removed thousands of videos and flagged content incorrectly. Many creators expressed frustration that AI could not understand context, leading to unfair penalties. According to a report on YouTube’s moderation, "Automated systems accounted for over 70% of takedowns, yet a significant portion of appeals resulted in reinstated content, highlighting AI's inability to differentiate between harmful and benign content" [1]. If Havoc Games were to use AI-based toxicity detection, there is a risk that things such as sarcasm, gaming slang, or cultural expressions could be wrongfully flagged as toxic. Players could receive unjust penalties or bans, damaging the game’s reputation and frustrating the player base. Content creators (streamers) could be negatively affected as well, if AI moderation wrongfully mutes or bans their content because this will impact their engagement and monetization. Especially since their style of content and freedom of expression will be extremely limited due to them being worried that they will be punished falsely.


(OKR1 Ethical Safeguards):

Bias Reduction: AI Fairness and Human Review System

To prevent things like AI bias and false positives there will be a system that certain AI flagged reports require human review before a penalty is given to a player. Not only that but there will be an appeal system that allows players to dispute a ban or mute done by AI which is reviewed by human moderators. This will be designed by AI ethicists, data scientists, and a community moderation team to handle player appeals and identify biases in AI detection. To implement such as system we would have to train and refine AI based on the false positives or negatives from the data to prevent future instances. We would also have to hire a specific team of moderators to handle case by case reviews and appeals. By integrating AI ethicists, community moderation teams, and linguistic experts in this process, Havoc Games aims to achieve a high AI-human agreement rate, with a goal of at least 90% accuracy in AI moderation decisions [2]. Not only that but we can do player surveys asking them for their opinions and feedback on the effectiveness of AI moderation.

(OKR2 Ethical Safeguards): 

  To alleviate the ethical impacts associated with our OKR, "Achieve a player retention rate of 75% over a 6-month period," we propose the following safeguards: Firstly, we will implement transparent data collection practices. This involves creating a clear and concise data collection policy, informing players about the types of data collected, stored, and used. The policy will be easily accessible within the game and on our website. The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) emphasizes the importance of transparency in data collection, stating that companies must "clearly and conspicuously" disclose their data collection practices [6]. Secondly, we will anonymize player data to prevent identification and protect sensitive information. This can be achieved through techniques like data encryption, hashing, and aggregation. The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) recommends anonymization as a technique to protect personal data [7].
  Thirdly, we will establish and enforce community guidelines that promote respectful and inclusive behavior. This will involve implementing a moderation system, where players can report toxic behavior, and our team will review and address these reports. The Entertainment Software Association (ESA) emphasizes the importance of community guidelines and moderation in maintaining a positive gaming environment [3]. Lastly, we will conduct regular security audits to identify vulnerabilities and ensure the protection of player data. This will involve collaborating with security experts and implementing recommendations from these audits. The OWASP Foundation recommends regular security audits as a best practice for protecting web applications [4].
  To design and implement these safeguards, we will involve a range of stakeholders, including ethical experts, context experts, and players. We will consult with experts in data protection, ethics, and gaming to ensure our safeguards align with industry best practices. We will also collaborate with game developers, community managers, and players to understand the gaming community's needs and concerns.To measure the effectiveness of these safeguards, we will track metrics such as player retention rate, community engagement and sentiment, number of reported toxic behavior incidents, and security audit results. By implementing these safeguards, we aim to create a positive and inclusive gaming community that prioritizes player well-being, fair gameplay, and community engagement

The company culture here at Havoc Games strives to be about curiosity, collaboration, adaptability, and celebration.
